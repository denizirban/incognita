SCIENTIFIC LIMITS

What these observations cannot establish

F146A, K211N or the double mutant may change local geometry and interface contacts. Static structures cannot establish Parkin activation, folding kinetics, cellular rescue, or Parkinson disease causality. MD requires correctly parameterized zinc sites, appropriate constructs and biochemical state, then independent replicas and convergence checks.
