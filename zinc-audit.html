02 / SCIENTIFIC GATE

What does a pass mean?

For each Zn atom, find donor atoms within 3.0 Å: cysteine SG, histidine ND1 or NE2. Report four closest donor atoms and their distances. A site with four distinct donor residues and donor distances of 1.8–2.8 Å is marked GEOMETRY PLAUSIBLE.

These cutoffs are screening heuristics, not a universal chemical criterion. They do not test oxidation states, tetrahedral angles, missing hydrogens, ligand protonation, bonded parameters, or functional activity. A pass never establishes MD readiness.

04 / LIMITATIONS

Zn sites may be absent from AlphaFold predictions or from model preparation. Missing Zn atoms do not prove that Parkin lacks zinc-binding domains. This tool does not infer zinc placement, create Zn–donor bonds or fix incomplete structures.
