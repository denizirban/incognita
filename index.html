THE SCIENTIFIC CONTRACT

Never confuse a model with reality.

Think of a protein as a moving machine, not a photograph. A structure is one observation or prediction of its shape. A molecular dynamics trajectory estimates possible motion under an explicit physical model. Neither alone establishes what happens in a living neuron.

01 / Observation

What has been measured? Preserve provenance, organism, experimental conditions and uncertainty.

02 / Model

Which assumptions connect measurements to predictions? Record force fields, solvent, protonation, sampling and random seeds.

03 / Test

What result would falsify the hypothesis? Compare controls, replicates and independent biological validation.

Incognita is a research and education prototype, not a diagnostic tool. Scientific summaries are curated starting points, not systematic reviews.
